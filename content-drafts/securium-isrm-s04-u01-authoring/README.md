# ISRM S04-U01 authoring draft

This directory is a review-only, unpublished authoring package for:

- course: `course-isrm`
- subject: `isrm-2025-2027-s04`
- learning unit: `isrm-2025-2027-s04-u01`
- questions: `isrm-q-v2-21`, `isrm-q-v2-22`

The official criteria anchors Subject IV, information-security management-system operation, and its broad major items of information-security risk-management planning and information-security risk assessment. It does not provide an official Securium U01 title or detailed objective set. The title and objectives in this package are Securium independent pedagogical content.

This unit continues Subject III by turning treatment, protection-measure, industry-context, and implementation-review results into a repeatable management-system operating plan. It teaches how to record scope, responsibility, cadence, method, evidence, and review triggers, then how to handle an ownership change as an operating gap and preserve decision lineage.

The scenarios and questions are independently authored and fictional. Official local PDFs are used only for bounded factual/reference anchors. `sourceExpressionReuse` is `0`; commercial PDFs, official sample-question wording, and legacy unbound candidate wording were not used.
