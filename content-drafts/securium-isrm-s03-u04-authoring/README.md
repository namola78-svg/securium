# ISRM S03-U04 authoring draft

This directory is a review-only, unpublished authoring package for:

- course: `course-isrm`
- subject: `isrm-2025-2027-s03`
- learning unit: `isrm-2025-2027-s03-u04`
- questions: `isrm-q-v2-19`, `isrm-q-v2-20`

The official criteria anchors Subject III-4, information-security-industry applied protection measures, and its three broad subitems. The title and objectives in this package are Securium independent pedagogical content; the Foundation does not provide an official U04 unit title or detailed objectives.

This unit continues S03-U01 through S03-U03 by applying treatment, operating responsibility, and implementation-review evidence to information-security-industry products and services. It teaches two bounded judgments: whether an industry-related designation or assurance claim fits the organization's actual risk, and how a provider/service-scope change can affect obligations, responsibility, evidence, and residual risk.

The scenarios and questions are independently authored and fictional. Official local PDFs are used only for bounded factual/reference anchors. `sourceExpressionReuse` is `0`; commercial PDFs, official sample-question wording, and legacy unbound candidate wording were not used.

The package is not canonical content, an approval record, a revision binding, a publication input, or a runtime registration. Canonical approval, source binding, permission for expression reuse, currentness beyond the available source evidence, runtime materialization, and publication remain unresolved or not requested.
