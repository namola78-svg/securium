# User Testing Plan

User testing should validate whether SECURIUM feels useful and trustworthy, not whether every future feature exists.

Detailed facilitator script: `docs/ui/preview-beta-test-script.md`.
Round 1 working log: `docs/ui/preview-beta-user-testing-round-1.md`.

## Participant profile

| Group | Count | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learners | 3-5 | Validate course selection, learning flow, practice, review, and analytics comprehension. |
| Admin operators | 1-2 | Validate curriculum, coverage, ontology, AI trace, and revision inspection workflows. |

## Learner tasks

1. Find a course that fits your goal.
2. Add or continue a course.
3. Open the curriculum and choose what to study next.
4. Complete or inspect a lesson.
5. Solve a practice question.
6. Read an AI explanation and identify its source/evidence area.
7. Find a wrong note or review item.
8. Interpret your analytics page.

## Admin tasks

1. Open the admin dashboard and identify the next operational priority.
2. Inspect curriculum coverage.
3. Find an ontology concept or edge and explain what it connects.
4. Inspect an AI trace and identify source context, citations, and safety notes.
5. Inspect a content revision and identify its impact.
6. Review a user question report.

## Observation prompts

- What did you expect this page to do?
- What would you click next?
- Which information felt trustworthy?
- Which label felt too technical?
- Where did you feel lost?
- Did any page look unfinished or internal?

## Success signals

- Learners can identify the next study action within 10 seconds on dashboard and curriculum pages.
- Learners understand that AI explanations are reference material, not official scoring.
- Admin users can explain the role of Inspector without instruction.
- Admin users can move between Coverage, Ontology, AI Trace, and Content Revision without needing a separate map.

## Record format

For each issue:

- Role: learner or admin.
- Route.
- Task.
- Expected behavior.
- Observed behavior.
- Severity: P0, P1, P2, P3.
- Suggested fix.
